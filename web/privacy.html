Addis Bites — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Contact: izzyblast2010@gmail.com

Addis Bites ("we", "us") operates a food-delivery marketplace connecting customers, restaurants and delivery couriers in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights, in line with the Ethiopian Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: phone number and display name (SMS OTP or Telegram login).
  • Orders: items ordered, delivery address details (sub-city, sefer, landmark, optional map pin / GPS), order totals and payment status.
  • Location: precise location only when you pin your delivery address or use courier features; used solely for dispatch.
  • Payments: handled by our payment provider (Chapa / Telebirr). We never store card numbers — only a payment reference and status.
  • Ratings & support: star ratings, review text, and dispute tickets you submit.
  • Device/diagnostics: crash logs (if enabled) to keep the app reliable.

2. How we use data

  • Creating and delivering your orders, and letting couriers find you.
  • SMS / Telegram notifications about your order (merchant confirmation, delivery).
  • Preventing fraud and abuse (e.g., price tampering, fake orders).
  • Aggregated analytics to improve service quality.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not share your phone number with other customers.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Sharing

Your order details (name, address, phone) are shared with the restaurant preparing your food and the courier delivering it — only for that order. Payment data is processed by our licensed payment provider under its own privacy terms.

5. Retention

Order records are retained for accounting and dispute resolution (up to 5 years as required by Ethiopian law). You may request deletion of your account and profile data at any time; legal-retention records will be minimised and anonymised.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your data, and object to processing, by emailing izzyblast2010@gmail.com. We respond within 72 hours.

7. Security

Tokens are signed (JWT) and transmitted over HTTPS. Secrets are stored encrypted in our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare). Delivery proof photos are stored only against the order record.

8. Children

Addis Bites is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We will notify users of material changes via the app or SMS before they take effect.
